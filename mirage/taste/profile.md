# Sello's listening profile (from his Apple Music export, 10 Oct 2026: 3,152 tracks, ~2,330 artists)

Method: parsed every entry (title — artist), counted artists, classified the most frequent ~170 artists by genre and
hand-checked a random sample of 90 entries for the long tail. Public audio-feature APIs (Deezer/iTunes/MusicBrainz/
AcousticBrainz) are not reachable from this environment, so tempo/key come from genre knowledge, not measurement.

## Centre of gravity (~60-65% of the library)
**Melodic deep house** — especially the Eastern-European / Russian deep scene (Jerro, Sharapov, Paul Lock, Papa Tin,
Nando Fortunato, Pete Bellis & Tommy, NICCKO, Housenick, Costa Mee, Mister Monj, Anton Ishutin, Korolova, Melih
Aydogan) — and **melodic / progressive / organic house** (Le Youth, Yoste, Sultan + Shepard, Nora En Pure, Lane 8,
Jody Wisternoff, Spada, Marc Philippe, COLIN, Diviners, The Distance, Shallou, HUGEL, WhoMadeWho).
Typical: 118-124 BPM, minor keys, 4-chord minor loops held 1-2 bars, lush warm pads, plucked synth/marimba-like
leads, deep round sub bass, soft side-chained pump, crisp but minimal drums (soft kick, clap/snap on 2 & 4, shuffled
16th hats, shakers), piano or Rhodes chords, chopped/airy vocal hooks, long builds, emotional breakdowns.

## Secondary
- Tropical / pop house & future bass (~8%): Kygo, Sigala, Sam Feldt, Jonas Blue, Lost Frequencies, ILLENIUM, Said The Sky.
- R&B / hip-hop / pop (~8%): August Alsina, Trey Songz, Chris Brown, Rihanna, T.I., Beyoncé, Katy Perry, Taylor Swift.
- Jazz / swing / classic vocal (~6%): Louis Armstrong, Miles Davis, Ella Fitzgerald, Dean Martin, Marilyn Monroe.
- Afro house / amapiano (~4%): Master KG, CryJaxx, Darque, ShaunMusiQ & Ftears.
- Melodic techno (small): Layton Giordani, Patrice Bäumel remixes.

## Implication for DJ Velvet Purple
The sound he loves is mostly **electronic production** (synth pads, plucks, sub bass, vocal chops) around real
piano/Rhodes/guitar/percussion — not orchestral strings, brass or xylophone. Style weights should lean heavily to
deep / melodic / progressive / afro-organic house.
