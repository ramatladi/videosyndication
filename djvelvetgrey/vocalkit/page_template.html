DJ Velvet Grey · Listening copy · Take 2

Real Instruments Set, take two

New versions of the 12 songs from 21 October. The titles, tempos and home notes are the same. The hooks, chord progressions, arrangements and lead instruments are new, with a different lead on every song, and every sound is a recorded instrument. Every song now carries CC0 vocals. Hooks and vocal parts were placed automatically and nobody has listened to these yet, so tell me which ones to keep, drop or redo.

    About this take

    Each song is 2:59, mastered to its style's loudness target and encoded at 192 kbps for listening. The Camelot code next to each key is for harmonic mixing; modal keys use the tonic's major or minor.

    Every song now has vocals, and each one has its own main vocal hook. All voices are CC0 (public domain) recordings from Freesound, so they are free for commercial use with no credit required. Each vocal was shifted into the song's key, chopped or stretched onto its tempo, and the lead instrument was pulled back to make room for it.
